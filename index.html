Privacy Policy

Dodgeball Duel
Effective date: November 12, 2026

Spica Studio ("we", "us") makes the mobile game Dodgeball Duel ("the game"). This policy explains what information the game and the services it uses collect, how it is used and what choices you have. If you have a question, write to spicastudio1@gmail.com.

1. What We Do Not Collect

The game has no account, no login and no chat. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. Your game progress (level, coins, gems, characters, skins, settings), the player name you type and the country you choose are stored only on your own device and are never sent to us. The name and flag are only shown to you inside the game.

2. Information Collected By Services Inside The Game

The game uses two services from other companies. They may collect information as described here:

Google AdMob (advertising). To show reward videos the AdMob SDK may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device and operating system information, app information and how you interact with ads. Google uses this to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy

RevenueCat and Google Play Billing (purchases). When you buy something, the purchase is handled by Google Play. RevenueCat helps us verify and restore purchases. It receives an anonymous app user ID, the product you bought, the time of purchase, the store receipt and basic device information. Learn more: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Google Play may also give us anonymous crash and performance reports (Android vitals).

3. How We Use Information

We use the information above only to run the game, to show reward videos you choose to watch, to process and restore purchases, to prevent fraud and abuse and to keep the game stable. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Consent And Your Choices

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other regions Google shows a consent message before ads are requested. You can change your choice later in Settings > AD PRIVACY SETTINGS (shown where it applies). On Android you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your phone settings (Settings > Google > Ads). Ads are optional: the game works without watching any video.

5. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, write to spicastudio1@gmail.com and we will help.

6. Data Sharing

We share information only with the service providers named in section 2, who process it for us or under their own privacy policies. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

7. Retention And Deletion

Game data stays on your device until you delete the game's data or uninstall it. Information held by Google or RevenueCat follows their retention rules. To ask us for access to, or deletion of, information connected to your purchases, write to spicastudio1@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example in the EEA, the UK or California) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your information and to object to its use.

8. Security

We use reputable services and do not store personal information ourselves. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. International Transfers

Google and RevenueCat may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States, under their own safeguards.

10. Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy. The new version will be available in the game (Settings > PRIVACY POLICY) with a new effective date.
